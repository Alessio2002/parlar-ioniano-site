What is Parlar Ioniano?

Parlar Ioniano is the native documentation portal for Ionian (Lingua ioniana), a detailed constructed romance language featuring three grammatical genders (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter) and comprehensive phonetic and grammatical paradigms[cite: 11, 15].

Lingua ioniana is a constructed language that has been developed independently from Latin, but since it is a romance language, it has many similarities to Latin and other romance languages[cite: 11, 15].

It is a language that has been developed for the purpose of communication and expression, and it has its own unique grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation rules[cite: 11, 15].
