What is Parlar Ioniano?

Parlar Ioniano is the native documentation portal for Ionian (Lingua ioniana), a detailed constructed romance language featuring three grammatical genders (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter) and comprehensive phonetic and grammatical paradigms.
